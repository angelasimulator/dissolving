Part 3

Part 3: Factors Affecting Dissolving

Review the key scientific factors that increase the speed of dissolving!

🔄

1. Stirring (Agitation)

• Effect: Stirring speeds up dissolving.
• Why it works: Stirring moves the water around! It carries dissolved parts away so fresh water can keep touching and breaking down the rest of the solid.

🌡️

2. Water Temperature

• Effect: Warmer water dissolves solute faster.
• Why it works: Hot water has lots of heat energy! Tiny water bits zoom around super fast and bump into the solid much harder and more often!

🧩

3. Particle Surface Area

• Effect: Smaller particle sizes dissolve faster.
• Why it works: Fine powder is crushed into tiny pieces! Water can touch and dissolve all the little grains at the exact same time, instead of waiting for a big lump to melt slowly.
